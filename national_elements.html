National Flag

The Ntional Flag is a horizontal tricolour of India saffron (kesaria) at the top, white in the middle and India green at the bottom in equal proportion.

National Anthem

The song Jana-gana-mana, composed in Bangla by Rabindranath Tagore.

National Song

The song Vande Mataram, composed in sanskrit by Bankimchandra Chatterji.

National Bird

the Indian peacock, pavo cristatus, the national bird of India
